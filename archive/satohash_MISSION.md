# Satohash — Mission

## Our Purpose
To make cryptographic proof of existence accessible to everyone — no account, no fee, no trust required. Satohash anchors document fingerprints to the Bitcoin blockchain, creating immutable, court-admissible proof that any file existed at a specific point in time.

## Core Values
1. **Zero-Knowledge** — Documents never leave your device. Only SHA-256 hashes touch the blockchain.
2. **Permissionless** — No account, no KYC, no gatekeepers.
3. **Verifiable Forever** — Proofs independently verifiable even if Satohash ceases to exist.
4. **Bitcoin Secured** — Anchored to the strongest proof-of-work network.

## The Problem We Solve
Traditional notarization is slow, expensive, location-dependent, and relies on trusted third parties.

## Our Solution
A 4-plane notarization mesh: SHA-256 hashing → OpenTimestamps → Bitcoin block anchoring → independent verification.

## Who It Serves
Legal professionals, creatives, researchers, corporations, and governments.
