## What Is Satohash?

**Satohash** is a Bitcoin-powered document timestamping platform that creates **cryptographic, courtroom-ready proof** that your digital files existed at a specific point in time — anchored permanently to the Bitcoin blockchain.

**No lawyers. No notary fees. No trust required.**

---

## How It Works

```
  YOUR DOCUMENT                 BITCOIN BLOCKCHAIN
       │                              │
       ▼                              ▼
  [ SHA-256 Hash ]  ──────────►  [ Block #XXXXXX ]
  (Your file never                (Permanent proof
   leaves your device)             in the world's most
                                   secure ledger)
```

1. **Upload** your document (we only see the fingerprint, never the file)
2. **Stamp** it to the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps
3. **Download** your `.ots` proof file — verifiable forever by anyone

## Why Bitcoin?

> *"Bitcoin's proof-of-work creates the most tamper-resistant timestamping system ever built. Every block is witnessed by thousands of nodes globally — no single entity can alter it."*

- **Immutable** — No one can change a confirmed Bitcoin block
- **Independent** — You can verify your proof without Satohash
- **Forever** — Your proof is valid as long as Bitcoin exists
- **Open standard** — OpenTimestamps works with any compatible verifier
