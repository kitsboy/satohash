## In one breath

Satohash lets you prove a file existed at a certain moment in time — **without ever handing the file to anyone.** Your document is read on your own device, a short "fingerprint" (a long number that uniquely represents the file) is written into the public **Bitcoin** ledger using an open standard called OpenTimestamps. Anyone can check that fingerprint, forever, with free tools. No account. No login. No one can delete or backdate it — not even us.

> **We can't read your file. We never had it. We just proved it existed, on Bitcoin.**

---

## What it honestly does

- **Proves a file existed by a certain date** — a contract, photo, manuscript, dataset, or invention.
- **Only a fingerprint ever touches the internet.** The file stays on your device.
- **The proof outlives Satohash.** If we disappeared tomorrow, your proof still verifies through the open Bitcoin network.
- **Independent.** You don't have to trust us — you can check the math yourself.

---

## What it honestly does NOT do (yet)

| We do NOT… | What that means |
|---|---|
| Prove *who* made a file | We prove *when* it existed. Proof of authorship (a private-key signature) is the next chapter — built, not yet shipped as a product claim. |
| Act like a notary or a lawyer | Not a notary, not a signature service, not a regulated timestamp authority. |
| File your taxes or satisfy a law | We strengthen evidence; we don't replace any regulated process. |
| Promise it will be "believed" in court | **Admissible, not presumed accurate.** Strong evidence — a court still weighs it. |
| Support anything but Bitcoin | **Bitcoin-only, forever.** No Ethereum, no other chains. |

## The five things we're building for (the premium story)

1. **Mergers & acquisitions** — prove data-room documents existed, when, and unaltered, without exposing the files.
2. **Family offices** — long-horizon records (trusts, wills, property) that stay verifiable across generations, even after the advisor is gone.
3. **Latin markets (Spanish & Portuguese)** — an independent existence-at-a-time layer alongside each country's official identity systems.
4. **Tax & compliance** — proof of what was on file before a fiscal deadline; strong evidence for audits.
5. **Business & enterprise** — the privacy-preserving integrity anchor; developer API for teams.
