How it works · zero-knowledge

Five steps to immutable proof

1️⃣

Hash

Your browser computes the fingerprint. The original bytes never leave your device.

2️⃣

Stamp

Only the 64-char hash goes to three independent OpenTimestamps calendars.

3️⃣

Anchor

Calendars merge hashes into a Merkle tree, committed to Bitcoin.

4️⃣

Confirm

~10 minutes later, a Bitcoin block permanently seals the commitment.

5️⃣

Prove

Download the .ots. Verify with open tools + a block explorer — forever.
