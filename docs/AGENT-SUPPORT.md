# Satohash support script

For agents answering a stuck stamp. Say this. Do not add to it.

## Say

- The file never left their device. We only received a fingerprint.
- Pending means the proof is on the way. Confirmed means a Bitcoin block sealed it. That usually takes about an hour, not seconds.
- We are not a lawyer. A stamp proves when, not who, and it does not replace a notary.
- Stamps are free. Do not create an invoice. Do not ask for a payment.
- If they need a person: kimi@giveabit.io

## Do not say

- Do not say Dark Vault encrypts the file. That switch is off. The file stays either way.
- Do not say satohash@giveabit.io. That name is not in the live list.
- Do not say the stamp is instant, court-ready, or zero-knowledge.
- Do not hold e-cash, open a mint, or send a payment link.

## Money (not live)

- Stamps are free. Do not create an invoice. Do not send a payment link.
- If a screen looks like a checkout, it is a demo. Say so. A demo must never look like a real payment.
- Any future sat we receive is labelled SATOHASH. Not a shared family label.
- A paid product waits on Lenny for the legal entity, and on Cam to say the word. Until then REQUIRE_LIGHTNING stays false.
- Refunds, if we ever charge, are new payments in sats. Write that rule before the first charge.
- Full note: docs/MONEY-RULES.md
