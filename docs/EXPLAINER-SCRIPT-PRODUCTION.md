# Satohash — 85-second explainer: production script

**Voice:** Kimi. Young English woman. HeyGen Pippa when that voice is available. Warm, wise, unhurried. A person who respects the listener.
**Tone:** Positive and clear. She explains a gift, not a threat. No rush. No sales voice. British English, modern, not posh.
**Music:** Sparse and dark, no vocals, no drums that fight the words. The bed sits well under the voice. Duck it whenever she speaks.
**Length:** About 85 seconds once she has room to breathe. The words below are the words she says.

**Do not say:** upload, zero-knowledge, courtroom, instant, paywall, who you are as a legal fact.

## TIMING AND SCENES

### 0:00–0:13 — HOOK (Kimi, then the three things worth keeping)
> "A photo. A promise. A page you wrote this morning. You should be able to prove it existed — and still keep it in your own hands."

### 0:13–0:24 — WHAT IS NOT A PROOF
> "Emailing a copy to yourself feels careful. It is not a proof anyone else can check."

### 0:24–0:38 — THE FINGERPRINT
> "Satohash takes the file on your phone or computer and makes a fingerprint. The file never leaves. We never see it. Only the fingerprint goes forward."

### 0:38–0:52 — BITCOIN
> "Independent calendars carry that fingerprint, and Bitcoin seals it inside one block. Anyone in the world can read that block. No account. And today, it is free."

### 0:52–1:08 — THE HONEST WAIT
> "Here is the part we will not rush. Pending means your stamp is on its way. Confirmed means a Bitcoin block has sealed it. Give it about an hour. Waiting is the strength."

### 1:08–1:25 — THE RECEIPT, AND THE CLOSE
> "You leave with a receipt, and a link anyone can open. Open tools can check it, even if we are no longer here. We prove when a file existed. We do not pretend to prove who you are. A confirmed proof is already waiting. Bitcoin block 969,627. Open it. Check it yourself. Then stamp your own. satohash.io."

## DELIVERY

- Smile in the voice on "keep it in your own hands," "free," and "check it yourself."
- Land **Pending** and **Confirmed** as two different words, with a breath between them.
- "We do not pretend to prove who you are" is kind and firm. Not a disclaimer read at speed.
- "969,627" is spoken as "nine six nine, six two seven," so the number stays clear and short.
- One breath at each scene boundary.
- The last word is the site, said like an invitation: "satohash dot eye oh."

## PICTURE

Institutional Noir. Deep navy. One gold accent, used for the stamp and the close, not for every label. Kimi on the open and the close (brown hair, gold eyes, white choker — the locked stills). Motion is diagrams: a file that stays on a device, a fingerprint that travels, three calendars, one block, then Pending beside Confirmed. The public example is block 969,627 and the start of hash `515b2a39`. Never show a filename.

The 10-second teaser stays as it is.
