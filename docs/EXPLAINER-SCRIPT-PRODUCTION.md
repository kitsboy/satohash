# Satohash — 60-Second Explainer: Production Script

**Voice:** Kimi (British English, warm & natural — NOT robotic)
**Tone:** Confident, clear, slightly conversational. Like a friendly expert showing you something cool.
**Music:** Low ambient drone + soft pulse. No drums. Volume -18dB under VO.
**SFX:** Soft whoosh on transitions. Clean "ding" for checkmark. Subtle block-chain clink for anchor.

## TIMING & SCENES

### 0:00–0:06 — HOOK (Scene: Hero stamp graphic)
> "Got a file that needs to exist *right now* — and be provable forever?"

[beat — soft whoosh]

> "Contracts, creative work, evidence... Satohash stamps it onto the Bitcoin blockchain. Permanently. For free."

### 0:07–0:18 — THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES (Scene: Document + stamp impact)
> "The old way? You email yourself a copy. Screenshot a timestamp. None of that holds up."
> "Satohash takes your file, creates a unique fingerprint, anchors it to Bitcoin — and gives you a receipt that can't be faked."

### 0:19–0:32 — HOW IT WORKS (Scene: Flow diagram — File → Hash → Calendar → Blockchain)
> "Upload to satohash.io. One click. No account. No wallet."
> "Your file's fingerprint is sent to three independent timestamp calendars. Once Bitcoin confirms a block — you're done."
> "That proof is yours forever. No subscription. No hidden fees."

### 0:33–0:42 — BATCH STAMPING (Scene: Multiple files → single anchor)
> "Got a folder of documents? Batch stamp them all at once."
> "Every file gets its own independent proof. Perfect for contract dumps, audit trails, or campaign records."

### 0:43–0:51 — VERIFY (Scene: Checkmark badge on document)
> "Need to prove it later? Come back, enter your stamp ID."
> "Satohash cross-checks the calendars and the blockchain. If it's real — green badge. Done."
> "Trust, but verify. That's the whole point."

### 0:52–0:60 — CTA (Scene: satohash.io + Bitcoin anchor visual)
> "Satohash is sovereign digital truth. Nothing to install. Nothing to buy."

> "satohash.io"

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **British female voice** — use ElevenLabs "Rachel" or "Bella" voice, NOT default TTS
- **Pacing:** Slight pauses between key sentences. Never rush.
- **Breaths:** Natural breath at every scene boundary (6 breaths total)
- **Emphasis:** "free" (warm), "can't be faked" (firm), "green badge" (satisfied)
- **Music:** Ambient pad in C minor, slow arpeggio. Loop the same 8-bar phrase. Fade in 0.5s, fade out 2s.
- **SFX:** 
  - Whoosh: 0:06 transition
  - Stamp impact + reverb: 0:09
  - Gentle ping on "green badge": 0:48
  - Subtle block clink on "Bitcoin": 0:54
