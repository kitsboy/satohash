## The idea, in one breath

**Satohash proves that a file existed, at a specific moment, permanently — by anchoring its fingerprint to the Bitcoin blockchain, without the file ever leaving your device.** It is a sovereign, open "civic notary": independent, verifiable-by-anyone proof of *what existed, when, and unaltered*. No middleman. No trust required.

> **"We don't need you to trust us. We need you to trust math."**

This is the Tim Berners-Lee idea: a small, open, non-commercial technology that helps the world hold truth. An open, auditable commons that anyone can verify, no platform in the middle.

## Why we are reaching out to you

We are honest about what we are not: we are **not** a company raising a profit-seeking round, and we are **not** a compliance product making legal promises. We are an **open-source research and civic-tools project** — building public infrastructure for governance, accountability, and truth — and we want it **hardened and improved by people who understand the stakes.**

Specifically, we seek:

### 🏛 For legal counsel & jurists
- **Review** of our positioning and evidence claims (we are meticulous about *not* overclaiming admissibility).
- **Guidance** on jurisdiction-specific standing (US ESIGN/UETA, EU eIDAS, LatAm notarization cultures) — where a Bitcoin-anchored timestamp genuinely *helps* evidence, and where it must *not* be presented as compliance.
- **Wisdom** on running an entity-less, free, open project ethically and safely (server in Germany; identity intentionally undisclosed pending product audit).

### 🛠 For technologists & cryptographers
- **Audit** of our OpenTimestamps integration, the Merkle-tree anchoring, and the (coming) private-key authorship layer.
- **Hardening** of the client-side hashing, the calendars, and the independent-verification path.
- **Contribution** to a genuinely open, self-hostable tool — FOSS, no vendor lock-in, no hidden backdoors.

### 💰 For funders, foundations & the philanthropic
- **Support** for an open civic-good project that uses Bitcoin's security to solve real problems — provenance, accountability, the fight against fake and synthetic media, whistleblower protection, archival truth.
- Funding that keeps the free anchor costs covered and helps us grow capacity — **research and public benefit, not profit.**
- If the gravity of what a global, verifiable, open proof-of-truth layer could mean for the world resonates — we would love to earn your partnership.

## Why this could matter for the world

In an age where anything can be re-tweeted but nothing can be trusted, the ability to prove *"this existed, at this time, unaltered — check it yourself"* is a civic utility. It protects:

- **Journalists & whistleblowers** — prove you had the story before you published.
- **Researchers & scientists** — pre-registration with teeth; no post-hoc data massaging.
- **Governments & archives** — public records that can't be quietly rewritten.
- **Citizens** — a receipt for "what happened when" that no platform can delete.

This is the Tim Berners-Lee idea: a piece of open infrastructure that helps the world tell and keep the truth. We want to build it well, harden it, and give it to the commons. **We'd rather show you old truth than new lies.**
