## What is Satohash?

Satohash is Bitcoin-native proof that a file existed at a moment in time. Your document never needs to leave your device — only a SHA-256 fingerprint is stamped via OpenTimestamps calendars and sealed into Bitcoin.

## Why use it every day?

- Seal contracts and drafts **before** disputes  
- Timestamp AI outputs and screenshots for provenance  
- Court-portable `.ots` proofs anyone can verify  
- Free tier live now (0 sats to you)

## How it works

1. **Hash** in the browser (Web Crypto)  
2. **Stamp** the hash to OTS calendars  
3. **Anchor** into a Bitcoin batch commitment  
4. **Verify** with open tools — no trust in Satohash required later
