# For counsel — what a Satohash stamp is (and is not)

Live: https://satohash.io/counsel

A Satohash stamp is a SHA-256 fingerprint attested via OpenTimestamps and later included in a Bitcoin block.

**It is** proof that a particular digest existed by the time of the Bitcoin attestation.

**It is not** proof of authorship, consent, truth, or a government notarial act.

Pending ≠ confirmed. Verify independently with `ots-cli`.
